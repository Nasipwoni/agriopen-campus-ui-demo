Offline First Design

Full access — even without internet.

AOC Digital Campus is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA). Download course modules at home, complete them on a matatu, sync results when you next get signal.

⬇️
Download once, learn anywhere
Star any course to save videos, PDFs, and quizzes to your device. No connection needed to study.
📤
Automatic sync queue
Quiz answers, forum posts, RPL evidence, and attendance are queued locally and synced automatically when you reconnect.
🔗
KNQA records preserved
Completed assessments sync to KNQA on reconnect. Your qualification is never lost — even if you're offline when you finish.
💬
SMS fallback notifications
When you're offline, deadlines and results reach you via SMS through Africa is Talking — no app required.
